Durable execution and the death of the cron job
A Postgres-backed queue can replace half of your infrastructure. When work must not be lost, a durable workflow beats a cron job on every axis that matters — retries, visibility, idempotency.
The idea is old but the ergonomics are finally good. River leans on the transactional guarantees you already trust, so enqueue-and-forget becomes a single statement inside the same commit as your business write.
“The database is the queue. There is no second system to reconcile.”
Batching is where the economics turn: group pending jobs, spend one API round-trip, take the discount. A bulk import of three thousand items simply grinds overnight.
AI summary
Durable, Postgres-backed queues replace cron for anything that must not be lost — retries, idempotency and batching included, no extra services.
