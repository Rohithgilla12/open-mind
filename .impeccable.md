# Design Context — Openmind

## Target audience
Individuals keeping a private, self-hostable "commonplace book" — thoughtful, literary-leaning knowledge workers who save links, notes, quotes, images, products, books, and videos and want them enriched, organised, and resurfaced by the machine. Self-hosted single-user by default; a small-group "cloud" flavour (a few friends) is the near-term hosted mode.

## Use cases
- **Capture anything, instantly** — a URL or a thought or an image, saved in one move; enrichment (summary, tags, colours, embeddings) happens async, in place.
- **Browse a beautiful collection** — the main "Mind" grid, type-aware cards, calm to sit in.
- **Find by fragment** — keyword, colour, or vibe, via hybrid FTS + vector search.
- **Revisit** — resurfacing (Drift) and a working set (Desk) — later milestones.

## Brand personality / tone
Warm, editorial, literary — **"a commonplace book, kept by a machine."** Print-inspired and tactile, not techy. It should feel like a well-set page, not a dashboard.

- **Palette (canonical — from `docs/design/README.md`, supersedes CLAUDE.md's cooler set):** warm canvas `#E4DDCD`, paper `#F4F0E6`, card surface `#FCFBF6`, ink `#1C1A16`, ink-muted `#57534A`, ink-faint `#A39C8B` (mono metadata), hairline `rgba(28,26,22,.10–.13)`. Accents: **cobalt `#1B3FD1`** (primary — buttons/links/active), **terracotta `#C24A2E`** (margin rule / editorial red hairline), **gold `#E0B23A`** (Drift/keep), green `#2E7D5B` (product/storage). Note surface `#FBF4D8`.
- **Type:** Newsreader (serif) for titles, quotes, editorial voice — italics used heavily. Instrument Sans for UI/body. JetBrains Mono for ALL metadata/tags/chrome (small, letter-spaced, often uppercase). Never plain system sans everywhere.
- **Signature detail (do not drop):** every card shows a row of small **palette dots** (~9px, 50% radius) = the item's dominant extracted colours. Core to the brand; reused as swatch filters in search.
- **Texture & depth:** warm paper with a faint horizontal-rule overlay; cards have a soft shadow + a `translateY(-2px)` hover lift; a 2px terracotta hairline tops the main view.

## What this is NOT
Not dark-mode-with-neon, not glassmorphism, not generic rounded-card-grid SaaS, not monospace-as-decoration. The mockups in `docs/design/*.dc.html` are the pixel-close reference to recreate in real Next.js (not to copy verbatim).
